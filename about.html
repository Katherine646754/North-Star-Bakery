About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery began with a simple idea: a neighborhood bakery should feel welcoming, familiar, and full of the smell of fresh bread. Our goal is to create handmade baked goods that bring people together during ordinary days and important celebrations.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We value quality ingredients and thoughtful preparation. Whenever possible, we choose ingredients that support freshness, reliable quality, and relationships with local and regional suppliers.
